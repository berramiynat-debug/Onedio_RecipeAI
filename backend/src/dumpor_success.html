FAQ

12 questions — from the basics to expert details. All answers are thorough and specific.

What is Dumpor and what is an Instagram Viewer?

Basics

Dumpor is a free, anonymous Instagram Viewer — a web tool that lets you browse public Instagram profiles, stories, reels, highlights, follower lists, and more without creating an account or logging in.

An Instagram Viewer works by routing your request through an anonymous proxy server. Dumpor fetches content using its own IP address and returns the public data to your browser. Instagram sees a proxy request — not your real IP, location, or identity. Your name never appears in a story viewers list, and your browsing is not linked to any advertising profile.

Dumpor provides 7 Instagram tools plus TikTok Viewer: Story Viewer, Story Downloader, Profile Viewer, Reels Viewer, Highlights Viewer, Tagged Viewer, and Follower & Following Viewer. All free — no "pro" tier, no limits.

Do I need to log in or create an Instagram account?

Basics

No account, no login, no sign-up of any kind. No Instagram account, no Google account, no email address, no phone number needed to use Dumpor.

Since 2022, Instagram redirects unauthenticated users to a login wall when viewing profiles, stories, and follower lists — even though that data is publicly available to logged-in users. Dumpor's proxy architecture gives you access to the same public data without forcing you into Meta's ecosystem.

Will the person know I viewed their story or profile?

Privacy

No — your view is completely invisible to the account owner.

Instagram shows story creators a list of people who viewed their story, populated from logged-in accounts only. Because Dumpor uses an anonymous proxy and you have no Instagram account connected, no view event is generated under your identity — your name never appears in anyone's viewers list.

The same applies to profile views and follower list access: Instagram has no notification mechanism for these, and Dumpor's proxy layer means no user-linked request ever reaches Meta's servers.

Can I see private Instagram accounts or stories?

Limits

No. Dumpor only works with public profiles. Private accounts have their content locked to approved followers — Dumpor fully respects this and does not attempt to bypass it.

One exception: profile photos are always public on Instagram, even for private accounts. Dumpor's Profile Photo Viewer can display the HD version of any account's avatar regardless of private/public status, because Instagram itself makes this publicly available.

Any service claiming to view private Instagram content is either misleading or operating outside Instagram's Terms of Service. Dumpor only accesses what's already public.

What exactly does Meta see when I use Dumpor?

Technical

Meta sees a request from one of Dumpor's proxy server IP addresses — that's it. No user agent linking to your browser, no cookies carrying your identity, no geographic signal from your real location, no session token from an Instagram login.

From Meta's perspective, the request is indistinguishable from any logged-in user browsing the same public profile. Meta cannot link the request to you personally, build a behavioral ad profile from it, or identify your device or location.

Does Dumpor store my search history or browsing data?

Technical

No personally identifiable data is stored. Dumpor does not log which profiles you searched, which content you viewed, your IP address, or any information that could identify you.

Aggregate, anonymized analytics — total view counts, total searches — power the live counters on the homepage. These are aggregate numbers with no user-level detail. See our Privacy Policy for the full technical breakdown.

What is follower/following ratio and why does it matter?

Analytics

Ratio = Followers ÷ Following. It's the fastest indicator of whether an account's audience was genuinely earned or built through follow-back tactics.

Lionel Messi: 503M followers, 272 following — ratio 1.85 million:1. That's organic authority built over years of real fame. Compare with an account having 500k followers and 490k following (ratio 1.02:1) — that's mass follow/unfollow strategy, not real influence.

Marketers use ratio as a first-pass filter before deeper engagement-rate analysis. Instagram hides the following list behind login since 2022 — which is why Dumpor's Follower & Following Viewer exists.

What's the difference between Instagram Stories and Highlights?

Tools

Stories expire after 24 hours — only visible during that window. Dumpor's Story Viewer accesses active, non-expired stories from public accounts in real time.

Highlights are story collections the account owner has permanently pinned to their profile. They never expire. Dumpor's Highlights Viewer shows all pinned folders and full content in HD — particularly useful for research and archival purposes.

Can I download Instagram stories and reels in HD?

Tools

Yes, for public profiles when the content is available in HD. Dumpor's Story Downloader saves Instagram stories as image or video files. Reels are available through the Reels Viewer without watermarks.

Quality depends on the original upload — if the creator uploaded in HD, you get HD. Dumpor does not re-compress content. Downloads are for public content only.

Is Dumpor legal to use?

Legal

Yes, for personal use accessing public content. Viewing public Instagram profiles, stories, and posts is legal — information the account owner has voluntarily made publicly available. No login bypass: Dumpor accesses only what any logged-in Instagram user can already see.

Prohibited under Dumpor's Terms of Use: using the service to harass or stalk, unauthorized bulk data collection, or commercially redistributing downloaded content. Dumpor is not affiliated with Meta Platforms or Instagram.

Can I use Dumpor for competitor monitoring and marketing research?

Marketing

Yes — this is one of the most common professional use cases. Viewing a competitor's public stories, reels, and posts through Dumpor is entirely anonymous. Their social media team won't see your brand account in their story viewers list.

Common marketing use cases: tracking competitor content cadence, monitoring influencer posting patterns before pitching, verifying follower/following ratio before a brand deal, checking a creator's tagged content ahead of partnership conversations.
